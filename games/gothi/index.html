The objective

How to Win

Win immediately by controlling five Farthings or by taking control of the opposing player's Homestead.

  • Add the control values of your uncovered pieces in each Farthing.
  • The higher total controls that Farthing; tied totals leave it neutral.
  • The two edge territories count as one Farthing only when you control both sides.
  • Covered pieces in a stack cannot move and contribute no control.

Piece lesson 1

The Gothi

The Gothi is your central leader and contributes 3 control to its Farthing.

  • Move one space in any direction.
  • Or move exactly two spaces in a straight line when the middle space is clear.
  • The two-space move is not a jump and cannot cross another piece or water.
  • A Gothi may capture with its one-space move, but not with its two-space move.

Your practice match has no opponent. Move your five Gothi into position until you control five Farthings or seize the empty Purple Homestead.

Piece lesson 2

The Raven

The Raven contributes 2 control—less than the Gothi's 3—but its freedom of movement makes it uniquely flexible.

  • Move one space in any direction.
  • Or jump exactly two spaces in a straight line.
  • The two-space jump may pass over pieces and water.
  • The Raven may land on and move through water spaces.
  • It may capture with either its one-space move or its two-space jump.

Piece lesson 3

The Thingman

The Thingman contributes 1 control and advances through short, flexible paths.

  • Move one space in any direction, then optionally move one more space.
  • The second step may turn in a different direction.
  • Thingmen cannot enter water.
  • A Thingman may capture an enemy occupying its final destination.

Piece lesson 4

The Outlaw

The Outlaw contributes 1 control but can cross long open lanes in a single move.

  • Move any number of spaces in one straight line.
  • The Outlaw stops at the board edge, water, or another piece.
  • It cannot jump over pieces or enter water.
  • It captures by moving onto the first enemy piece in its path.

The next practice board gives you only Ravens, Thingmen, and Outlaws. Purple pieces are scattered as stationary blockers and capture targets. Use these pieces to control five Farthings or seize the Purple Homestead.

Piece lesson 5

The Storgothi

The Storgothi is your most influential leader. It contributes 3 control to its Farthing and moves like an Outlaw.

  • Move any number of spaces in one straight line.
  • The Storgothi stops at the board edge, water, or another piece.
  • It cannot jump over pieces or enter water.
  • It captures by moving onto the first enemy piece in its path.

Your final practice game gives you a full Yellow lineup. Purple uses its normal Basic setup with two Gothi removed, and the computer plays back on Easy. Use everything you have learned to win.
